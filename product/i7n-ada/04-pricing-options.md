# 04. Tier 1 and Tier 2 Pricing Options

Status: internal proposal. Do not publish.

## Constraints

- Your hours cap Tier 2. Price per report has to cover your review time at a rate you'd accept.
- Tier 1 exists to make money and feed Tier 2. It must not compete with Tier 2.
- Target range for Tier 2: $1,000 to $5,000 (Section 8).
- Current public pricing: $199 to $1,999 per automated audit, by page count. Agency is contact-us.
- Customers are small and mid-size organizations and nonprofits. A $5,000 report is a board-level decision for a chapter nonprofit. A $1,500 report usually isn't.

## Option A. Keep current pricing, add Tier 2 above it

| Tier | Price |
|---|---|
| Free scan, 5 pages | $0 |
| Tier 1: automated audit | $199 to $1,999 by pages (current) |
| Tier 2: expert report | $1,500 / $2,500 / $4,500 by size |

- Pro: no change to the live site. Fastest to ship.
- Con: six automated price points next to three expert ones. Customers compare $1,999 automated to $1,500 expert and get confused.
- Con: a $1,999 automated audit for a large site pulls the buyer away from Tier 2.

## Option B. Simplify Tier 1, make Tier 2 the main product (recommended)

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free scan | $0 | 5 pages, results on screen |
| Tier 1: full automated scan | $149 (up to 100 pages), $299 (up to 500 pages) | Full-site scan, findings list, Excel export. No signature. |
| Tier 2: expert ADA report, Small | $1,500 | Up to 25 pages, 2 user flows, signed report, one verification rerun within 90 days |
| Tier 2: Standard | $2,500 | Up to 100 pages, 4 user flows, same terms |
| Tier 2: Large | $4,500 | Up to 250 pages, 6 user flows, same terms |
| Above 250 pages, multi-site, agencies | Quote | |

Tier 1 credit: the Tier 1 price counts toward Tier 2 if they upgrade within 30 days.

Rationale:

- Two Tier 1 prices are easy to choose between and cheap enough to buy on a card without approval. That's the job of Tier 1.
- Tier 1 drops in price on purpose. The value has moved to the signature. A high automated price says the scan is the product.
- The credit turns every Tier 1 buyer into a warm Tier 2 lead.
- Using the placeholder hours at the 3/5/8 target: $500 per hour for Small and Standard, about $560 for Large, before platform costs. At today's 6/10/16 hours, about $250 to $280 per hour.
- User flows priced in the tier, not just pages. Flows are where your manual time goes, and where demand letters point (donate, checkout, contact).

Risk: the live site loses its $399 to $1,999 automated prices. Anyone quoted from that table needs to be honored.

## Option C. Tier 2 only, scan free

| Tier | Price |
|---|---|
| Free scan, any size | $0 |
| Expert report | $1,500 / $2,500 / $4,500 |

- Pro: one decision for the buyer. Maximum push toward Tier 2.
- Con: no self-serve revenue. Section 2 says Tier 1 should earn revenue.
- Con: a free full-site scan has crawl costs and abuse risk.

## Rush pricing

Demand-letter customers have deadlines. Charge for it.

- Rush delivery (5 business days or fewer): plus 50%
- Only offer when your calendar has space. The intake form's deadline field drives this.

## Channel pricing (Elemental and agencies)

- Agency rate: 20% off Tier 2 list for agencies committing to 3 or more sites per quarter.
- The agency can resell at list. That's their margin and their reason to send work.
- Elemental's current contract terms stay as signed. Use the CCA work to set the agency rate, not the other way round.

## Retainer (for Section 10 discussion, not for launch)

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Monitor | $250/mo | Monthly automated scan, alert on new critical issues, remediation record kept current |
| Monitor + Review | $600/mo | Above, plus quarterly expert spot review and an updated signed report each quarter |
| Annual re-assessment | 60% of the original Tier 2 price | Full re-review, new signed report |

Offer the retainer at Tier 2 delivery, when the customer has just seen how much their site drifts.
