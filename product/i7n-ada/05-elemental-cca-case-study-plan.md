# 05. Case Study Capture Plan: Elemental Methods and CCA

Status: plan for CEO approval.

## Goal

One written case study and at least two testimonials, published within 30 days of the last CCA report shipping.

Use them in three places:

- Tier 2 offer block on the scan results page
- Agency outreach (the case for other agencies to resell)
- The i7n site and your LinkedIn

## Who's who

- Elemental Methods: the client. They contracted you.
- CCA Florida and CCA Mississippi: end beneficiaries. Their sites get assessed.
- The case study is about Elemental's outcome. The CCA chapters are proof that the work reached real organizations.

The agency angle is the one you want: "an agency added ADA assessments to its client work without hiring an accessibility team." That story sells the channel model.

## Step 0. Permission (do this first)

Before any work starts on the new report format:

- Ask Elemental in writing for permission to publish a case study naming them.
- Ask whether you may name the CCA chapters, or need Elemental to ask them.
- Agree on review rights: Elemental approves the final text before publishing.
- If naming isn't allowed, fallback: "a digital agency serving a national conservation nonprofit's state chapters".

Get this in email at minimum. A line in the next invoice or SOW is better.

## Step 1. Baseline (before remediation)

For each CCA site, record and keep:

- Date of the first scan
- Pages and flows in scope
- Issue counts by severity
- Criteria results: Supports, Partially supports, Does not support
- Screenshots of the three worst issues, e.g. a donate button with no label
- Your review time for the report, in minutes, broken out by: setup, review of automated findings, manual testing, writing notes, final checks

The time log matters as much as the case study. It's the first real data on report speed, which sets Tier 2 pricing.

## Step 2. During the work

- Keep the remediation record current: what was fixed, by whom, when, verified when.
- Note any slow step in your review. Each one becomes a build item for the reviewer screen.
- Save one or two moments worth quoting, e.g. an email from Elemental or a chapter lead reacting to a finding.

## Step 3. After the report ships

Interview Elemental's project lead. 20 minutes, recorded with permission.

Questions:

1. What made you look for ADA assessment work for CCA?
2. What did you try or consider before contacting i7n?
3. What surprised you in the first report?
4. How did the report change what your team or the chapters did next?
5. How did the chapters react?
6. What would you tell another agency considering this?
7. Would you do this again for other clients? Why?

Short interview with one chapter contact, if Elemental allows:

1. What did you know about website accessibility before this?
2. What changed on your site?
3. How do you feel about having a signed report on file?

## Step 4. Metrics to report

Use only numbers you can document.

- Issues found, by severity, per site
- Critical issues fixed and verified
- Criteria moved from "Does not support" to "Supports"
- Days from kickoff to signed report
- Number of pages and flows assessed

Do not report: lawsuit avoidance, legal outcomes, or traffic changes you can't attribute.
