# Task Brief Template

Nadia fills this in. One brief per task. Paste into the Agent Tasks row body.

```
TASK: [one line, verb first. Example: Draft LinkedIn post on why mid-project audits fail]

FUNNEL STAGE: [Aware | Engage | Subscribe]
NUMBER IT SHOULD MOVE: [Example: named targets reached, site visitors from LinkedIn]

COMMAND: [/write | /research | /rewrite | /repurpose | /linkedin-calendar | /landing-audit | none]

AUDIENCE: [Specific person. Example: COO at a 200-person services company who just lost a delivery lead]

INPUTS:
- [Topic Bank row, research file, prior draft, URL]

CONSTRAINTS:
- [Length, format, deadline, things to avoid]

DONE WHEN:
- [Concrete check. Example: 180 to 250 word post, one UTM link, file in drafts/linkedin/, scrubbed]

DUE: [date]
```

## Bad vs good

- Bad: "Write something about AI for LinkedIn."
- Good: "Draft a 200-word LinkedIn post for operations leads on the one AI task that saved our team a week. Use the Topic Bank row. Done when it has one UTM link and passes /scrub."
