## Linking Strategy

### Hub Linking Pattern

Every hub page should:
1. Link to 3-8 child pages in the intro or middle
2. Use descriptive anchor text that includes keywords where natural
3. Avoid over-linking (more than 8 internal links per page makes them all weaker)

## Linking Best Practices

1. **Anchor text**: Use descriptive phrases that include the target keyword when natural.
2. **Link depth**: Aim for every page to be reachable in 3-5 clicks from homepage.
3. **Link frequency**: 3-8 internal links per 1000 words of content is typical.
4. **Link density**: Don't over-link; quality > quantity.
5. **Relevance**: Only link when the connection makes sense to the reader.
