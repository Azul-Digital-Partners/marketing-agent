## Writing Mechanics

### Sentence structure

- Mix short and long. Two short sentences in a row are fine; three becomes staccato — break with a longer one.
- Active voice. "We [action]" not "The [action] is [done]."
- Cut hedge words: "[weak verb]" → "[strong verb]."
- Lead with the point. Topic sentence first. Don't bury the conclusion.

### Paragraph structure

- 1–4 sentences typical for web/blog content.
- Single-sentence paragraphs are OK for emphasis.
- One idea per paragraph. If you can't summarize it in one sentence, split it.

### Headings

- H1 once per page (the title). Sentence case: "How [your thing] actually works" not "How [Your Thing] Actually Works."
- H2 for major sections, H3 for sub-sections, H4 sparingly.
- Headings are scan-friendly: someone reading only the headings should understand the article's argument.

### Lists

- Use bullets for outcomes, deliverables, and scannable specs.
- Don't use bullets for narrative — that's prose territory.
- 3–7 items per list typical. More than 9 → group into sub-lists or split.
- Parallel structure: every bullet starts with the same part of speech (all verbs, all nouns, etc.).
